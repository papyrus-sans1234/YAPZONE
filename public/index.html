YAPZONE TERMS & CONDITIONS

1. Public Chatroom

YAPZONE is a shared public chatroom. Messages can be seen by other users. New users can also see previous messages in the shared chat history.

2. Protect Your Privacy

  • Do not share your real full name.
  • Do not share your home address or live location.
  • Do not share your school name, phone number or email.
  • Never share passwords or private account details.
  • Think carefully before sharing anything personal.

3. Respect Other Users

  • Do not bully, threaten or harass people.
  • Do not target someone because of their identity or beliefs.
  • Do not impersonate another person.
  • Do not deliberately spam the chat.

4. Prohibited Content

  • No threats or encouragement of real-world violence.
  • No sexually explicit content.
  • No sharing of illegal or harmful material.
  • No scams, phishing or attempts to steal accounts.

5. Accounts and Passwords

Keep your password private. Use a unique password that you do not use for school, email or other accounts. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account.

6. Chat History

Messages are stored so everyone can access the shared conversation history. Do not assume that a message can be permanently erased after sending it.

7. Moderation

YAPZONE may restrict access or take action against accounts that break these rules. Access to the service is not guaranteed.

8. Service Availability

YAPZONE may experience downtime, maintenance or technical problems. The service is provided as-is and may change over time.

9. Agreement

By creating an account, you confirm that you have read and agree to these Terms & Conditions.
